Social Grabber 隐私政策

生效日期 / Effective Date: 2026-09-03

本扩展 Social Grabber（"本扩展"）由 Social Grabber（"我们"）开发。我们非常重视你的隐私。本扩展的设计原则是：一切处理都在你的浏览器本地完成，我们接触不到任何你的数据。

1. 我们不收集任何数据

  • 我们不收集、不存储、不上传你的任何个人信息、浏览记录、下载记录或页面内容。
  • 本扩展没有任何服务器：不存在数据可以发往的地方。
  • 本扩展没有广告、统计分析（Analytics）、埋点或第三方追踪代码。

2. 保存在你浏览器本地的信息

本扩展仅保存你自己的偏好设置：

  • 下载目录名称（如 SocialGrabber）
  • 界面语言选择（中文 / English / 自动）

这些设置通过 Chrome 的 chrome.storage.sync 保存，并经由你自己的 Google 账户在你的浏览器之间同步。该数据完全归属你的 Google 账户，我们无法读取。卸载扩展即全部删除。

3. 权限用途说明

权限用途
downloads将你点击下载的媒体文件与文案 TXT 保存到你设定的下载目录
storage仅保存上述偏好设置
站点访问（抖音 / 小红书 / TikTok / Instagram / Pinterest 及其 CDN）在你浏览这些网站时，读取页面自身已加载的数据以识别当前作品的媒体地址；下载 CDN 上的媒体文件

4. 第三方网站

本扩展在上述第三方平台上运行，但与其无任何隶属关系。你在这些平台上的活动受其各自隐私政策约束。本扩展不会向这些平台或任何第三方发送额外请求（下载你主动点击保存的文件除外）。

5. 未成年人

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何能识别用户身份的数据。

6. 政策变更

如本政策发生变化，我们会在扩展更新说明与本页面同步公布变更内容。

7. 联系我们

Social Grabber Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-09-03

This policy applies to the browser extension Social Grabber ("the extension") developed by Social Grabber ("we"). The extension is built on one principle: everything happens locally in your browser — we have no access to any of your data.

1. We do not collect any data

  • We do not collect, store or transmit any personal information, browsing history, download history or page content.
  • The extension has no server: there is nowhere for data to go.
  • The extension contains no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party scripts.

2. Information stored locally in your browser

The extension stores only your own preferences:

  • Download folder names (e.g. SocialGrabber)
  • Interface language (中文 / English / Auto)

These settings are saved via Chrome's chrome.storage.sync and synced through your own Google account. The data belongs entirely to your account; we cannot read it. Uninstalling the extension removes all of it.

3. Permissions

PermissionPurpose
downloadsSave the media files and caption .txt you choose to download into your configured folders
storageStore only the preferences listed above
Site access (Douyin / Xiaohongshu / TikTok / Instagram / Pinterest and their CDNs)Read data already loaded by the page itself on these sites to identify media URLs of the post you are viewing; download media files from their CDNs

4. Third-party websites

The extension operates on the platforms listed above but is not affiliated with any of them. Your activity on those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies. The extension sends no requests to these platforms or to any third party other than downloading files you explicitly choose to save.

5. Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data that could identify any user.

6. Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page together with the extension update that introduces them.
